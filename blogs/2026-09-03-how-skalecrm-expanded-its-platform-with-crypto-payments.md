NOWPayments has joined forces with SkaleCRM, a CRM platform used by brokers and prop trading companies. SkaleCRM already brings client management, payments, and business processes into one system. The integration with NOWPayments adds another crypto payment provider to SkaleCRM’s ecosystem, giving clients more flexibility in how they accept digital assets.
