Robinhood Chain applications need reliable access to balances, contract state, transaction receipts, and live events. A slow or unavailable remote procedure call (RPC) endpoint can delay a wallet update, interrupt an indexer, or leave a trading interface waiting for fresh data. Robinhood Chain is an Ethereum-compatible layer 2 built on Arbitrum technology.
