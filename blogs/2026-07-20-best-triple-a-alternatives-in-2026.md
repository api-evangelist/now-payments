Triple-A is a licensed stablecoin payment institution that helps businesses collect, send, and convert digital currencies without directly holding crypto. Founded in Singapore in 2017, it has built a reputation for regulated fiat settlement, cross-border payouts, and its integration with Circle Payments Network. However, Triple-A does not fit every business.
