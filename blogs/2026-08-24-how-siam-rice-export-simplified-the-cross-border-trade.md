Overview Siam Rice Export Co., Ltd. is a rice exporter in Thailand serving Africa, the Middle East, and the rest of the world. As the company grew internationally, it sought to streamline the process of cross-border payment for buyers who experienced delays through traditional banking channels but also maintain efficiency in its own financial procedures.
