One of the simplest things about PayPal is that you can send money to someone using their email address. NOWPayments and ChangeNOW PRO bring that familiar interaction to crypto mass payouts: instead of collecting a wallet address from every recipient, businesses can send crypto using an email. Underneath, the infrastructure remains crypto-native.
