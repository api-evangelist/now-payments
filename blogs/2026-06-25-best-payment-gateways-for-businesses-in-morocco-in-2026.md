Payment gateways are essential tools in the world of e-commerce, facilitating online transactions between merchants and customers. They serve as a bridge, securely transferring data between the customer’s payment method and the merchant account. In Morocco, various payment options are available, including card payments through Visa and Mastercard, along with local debit card options.
