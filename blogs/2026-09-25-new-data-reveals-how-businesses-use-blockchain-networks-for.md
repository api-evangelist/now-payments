NOWPayments has released new data showing how businesses use different blockchain networks for crypto payouts. The analysis is based on six months of enterprise payout activity across major networks, including TRON, BNB Smart Chain, Solana, Bitcoin, and Ethereum. It looks at how these networks differ across transaction activity, payout volume, and processing speed.
