Collecting the first crypto payment is only part of running a subscription business. If customers have to open a new invoice and make another transfer every billing cycle, recurring revenue still depends on repeated manual payments. NOWPayments subscriptions let merchants create a billing plan once, add customers, and manage recurring payments from the dashboard.
