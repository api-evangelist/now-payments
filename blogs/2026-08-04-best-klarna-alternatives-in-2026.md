Klarna is a buy now, pay later (BNPL) provider that helps businesses offer installment checkout, pay-over-time financing, and pay-later options to shoppers. Founded in 2005 and headquartered in Sweden, it has built a reputation for brand recognition, global reach, and a wide merchant network. However, not every merchant finds Klarna to be the right fit.
